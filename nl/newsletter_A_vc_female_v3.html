Women are crushing the VC game!

Hello,


Welcome to Entrepreneurial Elevation. We connect founders to funding opportunities. This debut edition covers the capital now within reach for female entrepreneurs. Four initiatives are open: UBS, Female Founders Fund, Google, and The Artemis Fund. Each item below links to the details.

UBS Champions Women’s Wealth with Project Female Founder. UBS runs a global accelerator for early-stage, women-led ventures. The program lasts a year. It takes up to 100 female founders and pairs them with over 100 venture capital mentors. Founders spend eight weeks in an intensive accelerator, then twelve months in an activation phase. They get the tools, insights, and connections to scale and to raise institutional capital, including a Series A. Applications for the April 2027 cohort open soon. The program is part of the bank’s push for equal access to opportunity. See more.

Female Founders Fund (FFF) Closes $29M Fourth Fund. FFF is a seed-stage fund that invests exclusively in female-founded companies. The new fund brings total capital under management to $140 million. Its backers include Melinda French Gates’ Pivotal Ventures and the Anne Wojcicki Foundation. The firm has delivered three nine-figure exits: Billie, Eloquii, and BentoBox. Those returns are the case for gender-lens investing. FFF started in consumer. It now invests in enterprise SaaS, aerospace, and climate tech as well. The goal is $500 million into women-founded companies over the next decade. See more.

Google Empowers Entrepreneurs with the Women Founders Fund. Google for Startups built the fund to close the startup gender gap. It backs early-stage companies, pre-seed to Series A, with at least one full-time female founder. Selected companies receive up to $100,000 in equity-free cash and give up no ownership. They also get Google Cloud credits, custom mentorship from industry experts, and research-based leadership coaching. An alumni network and dedicated product support carry founders through each stage of growth. See more.

Fueling the Next Generation of Female Founders. The Artemis Fund is a women-led VC firm. It makes targeted seed-stage investments in tech-enabled, female-founded companies. In 2025 it added six startups to its portfolio, across care tech, commerce, fintech, and the future of work. Its network helped portfolio companies raise $55 million in total capital last year. Three female partners launched the firm seven years ago. They have closed two funds: $15 million in 2021 and $36 million in 2024. The mission is to close the tech funding gap for female entrepreneurs. See more.

Let’s rally behind these initiatives, and we hope they will benefit your potential entrepreneurial ventures. Until next time, keep empowering and stay inspired!
