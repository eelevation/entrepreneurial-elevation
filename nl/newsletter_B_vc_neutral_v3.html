Fueling the Next Wave of Innovation!

Hello,


Welcome to Entrepreneurial Elevation. We connect founders to funding opportunities. This edition covers the capital now within reach for early-stage and growth startups. Four initiatives are open: AWS, General Catalyst, Salesforce Ventures, and Andreessen Horowitz. Each item below links to the details.

AWS Generative AI Accelerator Continues to Fuel AI Innovation. The AWS Generative AI Accelerator is one of the most sought-after programs for generative and agentic AI startups. It is a global, eight-week hybrid program, and the cohort is invite-only. Selected startups receive up to $1 million in AWS credits. They also get dedicated technical and go-to-market mentorship, and they present at AWS re:Invent. The program targets founders working on hard technical problems, from pre-training foundation models to critical AI infrastructure. Applications for the most recent cohort have closed. If you build in AI, watch for the next cycle. See more.

General Catalyst Executes on Global Resilience Strategy. General Catalyst raised $8 billion in 2024. It is now deploying that capital against its Global Resilience thesis. Supply chains and geopolitics remain complex in 2026. The firm is directing resources outside traditional software, to founders building critical infrastructure across defense, healthcare, energy, and manufacturing. The strategy reflects a wider shift among top-tier VCs toward companies solving real-world problems. After a strong start to 2026, the firm is seeking new investments. See more.

Salesforce Ventures Rapidly Deploys its $1 Billion AI Fund. Salesforce Ventures doubled its Generative AI Fund to $1 billion. It then deployed over $850 million by late 2025. The fund has backed 35 AI-focused companies with a combined valuation above $270 billion. Its first investments went to Anthropic and Cohere. The portfolio now includes Runway, ElevenLabs, and World Labs. The strategy is to build an ecosystem of enterprise innovators that integrate with the broader tech stack. For founders, it is one of the largest corporate sources of AI capital. See more.

a16z Deploys $7.2B War Chest into Open Source and AI Infrastructure. Andreessen Horowitz raised $7.2 billion in 2024. It is deploying that capital across its American Dynamism, infrastructure, and apps vehicles. A main focus is open-source AI and infrastructure. The firm published its thesis plainly: open source is the only way forward for AI development. The fund has let a16z lead large rounds for AI infrastructure and foundation model companies. The firm is now one of the most active investors in the maturing AI stack. See more.

Let’s rally behind these initiatives. We hope they benefit your own ventures. Until next time, keep building.
